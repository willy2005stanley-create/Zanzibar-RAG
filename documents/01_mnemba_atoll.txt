Mnemba Atoll

Mnemba Atoll off the northeast coast, near Nungwi and Matemwe, is Zanzibar's best-known snorkeling and diving spot. It's a protected marine conservation area with clear water, coral reef walls, sea turtles, and reef fish. Most visitors reach it by boat on a half-day trip from nearby beach towns. A conservation fee is charged separately from the boat trip cost.
