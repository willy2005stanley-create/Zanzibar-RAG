Kizimkazi dolphin tours

Kizimkazi on the south coast is the main departure point for dolphin-watching and swim-with-dolphin trips, home to resident pods of bottlenose and humpback dolphins. Early morning boats have the best chance of sightings before the dolphins move further out. Some operators are gentler on the dolphins than others; choosing a guide who limits boat numbers and doesn't chase pods is kinder to the animals.
